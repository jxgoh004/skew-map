      How this is made

      • Skew = (25-delta put IV − 25-delta call IV) ÷ at-the-money IV, on the standard monthly expiry 25–60 days out. Positive means protection is bid; negative means upside is bid.
      • Options data: Cboe delayed quotes (per-contract implied volatility and delta, about 15 minutes delayed). Prices, volume, earnings dates: Yahoo Finance. Both are free and unofficial, with no uptime guarantee.
      • Sector rank compares a name only with its own sector group. Δ skew compares with the snapshot at least five days older, and stays blank until that history exists — the level is structural, the change is the signal.
      • Index put/call ratios are deliberately not shown: they depend almost entirely on which strikes are counted.
      • Earnings inside the expiry are flagged EVT — elevated IV there is event premium, not sentiment.
      • Relative volume compares the latest session with its 20-day average; a run before the close understates it.
